## 2. What this means for the product (agent's assessment, not legal/policy advice)
- **MVP (Watch + Guide) does not need AccessibilityService.** It uses MediaProjection, overlays, intents and push. This validates the MVP scope.
- **Act mode is the product's biggest platform risk.** Tap/type remote control normally needs AccessibilityService. Policy item 1 and the Android 17 change could block or limit it. Decide a fallback early (see 4).
- **AI Angel acting autonomously is directly in the policy's crosshairs.** The PRD's AI Angel (plan -> act) conflicts with the "autonomously initiates, plans and executes" wording unless a human approves every step AND Play accepts that reading. Treat AI "Act" as at-risk; AI that only suggests Guide overlays and shortcuts is safer.
- **Session consent per capture** (item 5) fits Angel's consent model; build the UX around the Android system prompt appearing each session.
